Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone


Harry Potter is a world-famous fantasy movie and book series based on the novels by J.K. Rowling. It follows the magical journey of a young wizard named Harry Potter as he discovers his identity and attends Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry.



Main Characters
Harry Potter - The Boy Who Lived and the main protagonist.
Hermione Granger - The brightest witch of her age and Harry's loyal friend.
Ron Weasley - Harry's best friend who is always there through adventures.
Lord Voldemort- The dark wizard and the main antagonist.



Movie Details
enre: Fantasy / Adventure / Magic
Setting: Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry
Key Themes: Friendship, Bravery, Good vs. Evil
